# distill research ledger

## Source 1

Title: Easy Approach to Requirements Syntax (EARS)

Author / Organization: Alistair Mavin et al.

Publication date: 2009

Version: IEEE RE 2009 paper

URL: https://doi.org/10.1109/RE.2009.9

Source type: primary research

What it establishes:
A small set of structured patterns can reduce ambiguity in natural-language requirements.

What this skill adopts:
Capture actor, trigger, precondition, response, failure behavior, and observable acceptance without forcing every sentence into a template.

What it does NOT establish:
It does not prove that all requirements should use EARS or that structured syntax replaces stakeholder decisions.

Numbers taken from source:
No numerical threshold is adopted.

Reverify when:
A newer authoritative requirements method changes the adopted practice.

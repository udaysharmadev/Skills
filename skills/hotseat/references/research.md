## Source 1

Title: Improving factuality and reasoning in language models through multiagent debate

Author / Organization: Yilun Du et al.

Publication date: 2023

Version: arXiv:2305.14325

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14325

Source type: primary research

What it establishes:
Independent model instances can expose errors through debate in the evaluated settings.

What this skill adopts:
Collect independent judgments before synthesis and preserve disagreement that evidence does not resolve.

What it does NOT establish:
It does not prove that named personas, fixed panel sizes, or voting guarantees better decisions.

Numbers taken from source:
No numerical threshold is adopted.

Reverify when:
Materially different replications or model behavior appear.

Title: Refactoring

Author / Organization: Martin Fowler

Publication date: Living catalog

Version: Second-edition catalog website

What it establishes:
Small behavior-preserving transformations can improve internal structure without changing observable behavior.

What this skill adopts:

What it does NOT establish:
It does not justify broad rewrites or guarantee behavior preservation without evidence.
